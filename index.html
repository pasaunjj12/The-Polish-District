Before You Book

Please review our booking policies before requesting your appointment.

Deposits

A $20 nonrefundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. Your deposit will be applied toward your total service cost.

Late Arrivals

We allow a 10-minute grace period. Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule, and the deposit may be forfeited.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Deposits are nonrefundable.

No-Shows

Clients who miss their appointments without notice may be required to pay a new deposit before booking again.

Additional Guests

To maintain a relaxing salon environment, please do not bring additional guests or children unless approved in advance.

Nail Inspiration

Please upload your inspiration photos when requesting your appointment. Detailed designs may require additional time and pricing.
